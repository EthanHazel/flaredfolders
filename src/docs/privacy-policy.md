## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information from visitors. This includes (but isn't limited to):

- Names, email addresses, or contact details
- Account or login information
- Payment or financial information
- Location data
- Behavioral or tracking data (e.g. browsing habits, click patterns, analytics profiles)

## 2. Server Logs

We do not run any analytics or tracking software. However, like nearly all websites, this site is served through hosting infrastructure that may automatically record basic technical information as a normal part of operating a web server. For example, IP address, browser type, and the date/time of a request. This is standard server behavior used for maintaining security and stability, not for identifying or profiling individual visitors, and we do not access, analyze, or use these logs for any other purpose.

## 3. Cookies

We do not use cookies of any kind. No first-party cookies and no third-party cookies.

## 4. Third-Party Services

This website is fully self-contained. We do not embed or rely on third-party services, scripts, fonts, media players, ad networks, or content delivery networks that could collect data about you.

## 5. Advertising

We do not use advertising networks, and no advertisements are displayed on this site. As a non-profit project, no revenue is generated through user data or any other means.

## 6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## 7. International Visitors (EU/UK/GDPR)

This site is accessible to a global audience, including visitors from the European Union and United Kingdom. Because we do not collect or process personal data beyond the minimal, non-identifying server logs described in Section 2, there is no data collection here requiring your consent or a legal basis for processing under the GDPR or UK GDPR. If you have questions about how this affects your rights as a data subject, you're welcome to contact us using the details below.

## 8. Links to Other Websites

If this site links to external websites, we are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
